# Orbit Store 0.7.0 (Beta)

**A big update: massively improved download speeds in Orbit Store.**

- **Faster downloads.** A reworked download engine makes better use of your connection and storage.
- **Reliable pause and resume.** Keep your progress when pausing or restarting Orbit. Existing compatible partial downloads remain resumable.
- **Native app improvements.** Manage sources, default storage, pairing and catalogue refresh from App settings. Check and update the app and download service separately, with notifications when updates are available.
- **Browser improvements.**

After upgrading, keep using Orbit 0.7.0 or later. Older releases cannot read the updated download queue.
